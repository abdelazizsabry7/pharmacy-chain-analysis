# Dataset

This folder contains the synthetic CSV files used in the pharmacy chain analysis.

## Files

- `customers.csv`: customer demographics and registration information.
- `products.csv`: product names, categories, cost prices, and retail prices.
- `inventory.csv`: current stock, reorder levels, safety stock, lead times, and average daily sales.
- `transactions.csv`: transaction dates, customers, products, quantities, prices, stores, and holiday status.

The dataset was generated for educational and portfolio purposes and does not contain real customer or business information.
